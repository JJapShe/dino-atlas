## Evidence boundary

- The holotype GI SPS 100/1501 is the only known specimen.
- The skull roof is complete and described as tablelike, slightly depressed
  centrally, and narrowed just before the postorbitals. The medial roof is
  weakly ornamented; stronger node-like ornament lies along the rear
  squamosal margin. The front of the nasal bears pointed tubers.
- The shallow elongate premaxilla has three caniniform teeth, followed by a
  large diastema and at least 15 maxillary cheek teeth. The first mandibular
  tooth is also enlarged and caniniform. Gallery portraits must not replace
  this heterodonty with a uniform predator tooth row.
- The postcranial material includes four weakly coossified sacrals, a nearly
  complete series of 32 caudal centra with tendons, ilia, partial forelimbs,
  distal hindlimb elements, metatarsals II-IV, toe elements, and pedal
  unguals. The preserved pedal claws are robust, triangular, and not
  recurved.
- Only indeterminate manual phalanges are preserved. A five-digit hand is a
  conservative comparative reconstruction, not a directly demonstrated
  species-level count. Full-body images therefore use readable hands but do
  not present exact manual phalangeal anatomy as proven.
- The type horizon is Upper Cretaceous red sandstone at Boro Khovil, South
  Gobi. The original paper states that its precise age is undetermined.
  Gallery captions must not relabel it as a secure Nemegt, Djadokhta, or
  Baruungoyot association or invent a confirmed same-frame fauna.
- Colors, skin, vegetation placement, gait, feeding, sociality, sex, and
  maturity are reconstruction hypotheses.

## Taxonomic and ontogenetic caution

Schott et al. 2011 discuss the possibility that flat-headed pachycephalosaurs
such as Goyocephale could represent juveniles, but also note that open sutures
and supratemporal fenestrae may be primitive adult features. Larger samples
and independent evidence are needed:

https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0021092

The atlas therefore keeps `Goyocephale lattimorei` as the working taxon while
avoiding claims about a known adult body form, sex, pair bond, or growth stage.

intentional crop that makes no limb or tail claim.
