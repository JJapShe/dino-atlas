# Adasaurus mongoliensis reference notes

This folder supports anatomy-led review for the six-image `Adasaurus mongoliensis` gallery rebuild. Published fossil figures are evidence controls, not style targets.

## Evidence boundary used in the gallery

- Directly supported: a medium-sized dromaeosaurid; a dorsoventrally expanded maxillary process of the jugal; a strongly anteriorly curved descending lacrimal process; a large surangular foramen; a dorsally displaced triangular quadrate process; pleurocoels restricted to anterior sacrals; a notched anterior ilium; and a strongly reduced pedal digit-II ungual.
- Foot gate: the digit-II ungual must remain small and approximately comparable to the other toe claws. The gallery must not substitute the enlarged `Deinonychus` or generic movie-raptor sickle.
- Preservational boundary: the skull is incomplete anterior to the preorbital bar, and the holotype lacks the forelimbs. Full snout shape, three-fingered hands, arm feathering, body plumage, and life colors are comparative dromaeosaurid reconstructions rather than direct `Adasaurus` soft-tissue evidence.
- Specimen-label caution: the Turner text and figure caption do not use the same specimen number consistently for the complete pes. The gallery therefore treats the published foot morphology as taxon-level evidence without asserting a specimen number in visible copy.
- Habitat reconstruction: the Nemegt scene uses broad seasonal channels, point bars, exposed sand and mud, driftwood, sparse horsetails and ferns, and open conifer woodland. Exact water level, season, weather, and plant placement remain hypotheses.
- Behavior and association: the channel-separated `Saurolophus` and two-adult scenes show regional coexistence or proximity only. They do not establish predation, pursuit, pack behavior, pair bonding, sex, courtship, kinship, hierarchy, or coordinated movement.

## Stable phenotype

- Canonical: smoky warm-gray and muted taupe body feathers, pale flax underside, broad low-saturation mineral-green shoulder field, restrained burnt-sienna cheek and nape, dark petrol forearm feathers, warm umber-gray lower legs, matte charcoal snout, and horn-colored claws.
- Color-pattern variant: soot-indigo to smoky violet-gray dorsum, dusty teal shoulder and flank field, pale flax underside, muted copper cheek and nape, dark plum-petrol forearms, and umber-gray lower legs.
- Reject regular tail rings, zebra bars, dots, checks, fluorescent accents, or a uniform global color cast.

## Six gallery roles

1. Neutral whole-body representative with the reduced digit-II condition kept modest.
2. Reverse-facing blue-hour color and pattern variant.
3. Wide Nemegt seasonal point-bar habitat.
4. Low-angle identity view that makes the small digit-II ungual readable without labels.
5. Channel-separated regional context with one distant `Saurolophus`-like hadrosaur.
6. Two spatially separated adults showing restrained intraspecific color variation.

## Rejection gates

- Reject modern turkey, eagle, or generic bird heads; toothless beaks; broad modern wing fans; or hands that do not resolve to three digits when readable.
- Reject a giant elevated sickle claw, fused toes, extra toes that cannot be mapped to digits I-IV, detached feet, extra legs, duplicated arms, multiple tails, or a tail cropped before its tip.
- Reject crowded ecology frames whose animals overlap or cannot be counted independently.
- The former three project images are superseded for bird-like heads, broad wing-fan arms, regular tail rings, weak reduced-digit-II readability, and unstable multi-animal appendage counts.
