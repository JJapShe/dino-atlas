## Evidence boundary

- `Conchoraptor` is a small oviraptorid with no pronounced external cranial
  crest. The dorsal skull table is flat rather than smoothly rounded like
  `Khaan`.
- A developed transverse nuchal crest rises above the skull table, producing
  an abrupt, near-right-angle transition between the dorsal and posterior
  surfaces.
- The premaxilla has a near-vertical anterior margin and a shallow
  posterodorsal nasal process. The toothless beak is broad and U-shaped.
- The jugal is more robust and anteroposteriorly shorter than in `Khaan`, with
  a nearly horizontal ventral margin.
- The hand has three clawed digits. Digit I is enlarged and robust; digits II
  and III remain unreduced, straighter, and more slender.
- Pedal digit III is longest, followed by II and IV. The pedal ungual of digit
  II is longest but is only weakly curved; do not add a dromaeosaur sickle
  claw.
- `IGM 100/1203` preserves 13 caudals, but its distal tail is missing.
  Oviraptorosaur comparisons support a compact tail, not a species-level
  `Conchoraptor` pygostyle or large fan claim.
- `IGM 100/3006` preserves a partial rostrum, braincase, and much of the
  postcranium but lacks the forelimb and parts of the vertebral column.
  `IGM 100/1203` is a nearly complete articulated postcranium without a
  skull. Whole-animal reconstruction therefore combines compatible
  species-level evidence rather than presenting one complete skeleton.
- A widely referenced mounted composite joined the `Conchoraptor` holotype
  cranium to `Heyuannia` postcrania. Do not use that mount as a whole-body
  reference.
- Body feathers, arm feathers, skin, color, pattern, and living behavior are
  comparative oviraptorosaur reconstructions, not direct `Conchoraptor`
  soft-tissue evidence.

## Hard identity gates

- One short, deep, boxy, crestless skull with a visibly flat roof and an
  abrupt raised nuchal step at the rear.
- One robust cheek below and behind the large orbit. Reject a smooth parrot,
  cassowary, ostrich, turkey, lizard, or generic modern-bird head.
- One broad U-shaped toothless beak with a near-vertical front margin.
  Reject visible teeth, a narrow hooked bird bill, or a false second mouth.
- No tall crest, casque, horn, or domed `Khaan`-like crown.
- Exactly two forelimbs. Each readable hand has exactly three total digits:
  one short robust digit I and two longer slender digits II and III.
- Exactly two hind limbs and two separate feet in complete-body views.
- No enlarged dromaeosaur-like sickle claw.
- One compact continuous tail originating at the pelvis. Reject a second,
  forked, detached, streamer-like, or abruptly cropped tail.
- Do not present a large terminal fan or species-level pygostyle as direct
  evidence.
- Reject extra heads, arms, hands, legs, feet, fingers, tails, or duplicated
  joints.

## Habitat and behavior gates

- Use the Baruungoyot Formation at Khulsan as red, well-sorted aeolian
  sandstone with dune and interdune deposits, broad open ground, and sparse
  non-flowering vegetation.
- Rare post-rain water films and shallow runoff channels are restrained
  reconstructions. Do not turn them into permanent wetlands.
- Avoid modern grassland, flowers, palms, cacti, modern shrub desert,
  infrastructure, or a globally orange color cast.
- The two newly described specimens may have been buried together during a
  dune collapse. This taphonomic association does not establish pair bonding,
  courtship, sex, mating, family structure, or stable social behavior.
- A distant armored herbivore may be used only as unidentified regional
  community context. Do not label it as a particular species or imply
  pursuit, predation, combat, or direct association.
- Do not use shells, fruit, eggs, or a specific food item to claim diet.
  Strong cranial performance does not by itself prove shell crushing.

## Consistent phenotype

- Dorsal body plumage: deep charcoal blue-gray.
- Underside: warm buff.
- Cheek and lower-neck accent: muted rust red.
- Shoulder zone: subdued ochre to mineral green.
- Forelimb feathers: graphite.
- Beak: matte horn gray; lower legs: dark scaled gray.
- Pattern: broad irregular low-saturation color zones with only fine natural
  mottling.
- No regular dots, rings, stripes, checkerboard, neon cyan, or neon magenta.
- Keep only subtle contour feathering on the compact tail; no peacock fan.

## Six gallery roles

1. Neutral complete-body representative showing the flat roof, nuchal step,
   three-digit hands, two feet, and one compact tail.
2. Reverse-facing dawn walk preserving the same phenotype and anatomy.
3. Wide post-rain Khulsan dune-and-interdune habitat reconstruction.
4. Intentional head-to-hand crop showing the flat roof, nuchal step, robust
   cheek, vertical-front beak, and exactly three digits on each hand.
5. One full-body `Conchoraptor` with one distant unidentified ankylosaurid as
   cautious regional community context.
6. Two fully separated `Conchoraptor` adults as cautious proximity context,
   with no pair, sex, courtship, reproduction, or sociality claim.
