## User-reference boundary

Two user-provided Opabinia images were used only to clarify that the five eyes should cluster on the head rather than form a line, and that the opposed proboscis tips should show an inward fringe of short spines or bristles. Their style, pose, texture, palette and background were not requested or copied, and neither file is distributed or referenced by the app.
