# Dinosaur Anatomy Audit

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This audit checks the current app-connected gallery images against a stricter anatomy gate. The goal is to prevent visually pleasing but structurally wrong renders from entering the suitable-candidate pool.

## Status Labels

- `count-level pass`: no obvious tail-count or leg-count issue at thumbnail scale; still needs paleontology review before final release.
- `anatomy review`: plausible candidate, but hands, feet, crop, occlusion, or taxon-specific traits need closer review.
- `structure reference`: useful as a shape or diagnostic guide, not a final natural image.
- `diagnostic only`: keep as a failure/comparison image; do not promote to primary candidate.

## Gate Checklist

Every candidate must clear these before it can be treated as suitable:

- one visible tail unless the tail is intentionally hidden and documented
- correct stance and visible limb count for the taxon
- no extra tail-like artifacts, background branches, or shadows reading as limbs
- no cropped head, tail, feet, or diagnostic anatomy in a way that blocks review
- reference cross-check against at least one reliable natural-history source before promotion; visual polish cannot override a reference mismatch
- taxon-specific traits: T. rex two fingers, Allosaurus longer three-finger forelimbs, Velociraptor feathering and sickle-claw cue, Triceratops three horns, beak, skull-attached frill, and non-hoofed ceratopsian body, Stegosaurus dorsal plates and four-spike thagomizer, Ankylosaurus low armor and tail club, sauropod shoulder/neck profile
